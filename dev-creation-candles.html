How to use your sachet

A few simple steps to get the most out of every Dev Creation wax sachet — more fragrance, longer life.

01 · Placement

Small, enclosed spaces work best

Wardrobes, shoe cabinets, drawers, car dashboards, washrooms. The fragrance concentrates in confined spaces and creates a lasting impression every time you open the door.

02 · Duration

Enjoy 4–6 weeks of fragrance

Each sachet is engineered to release scent steadily over weeks. Cooler environments extend the life further. Simply replace when the aroma fades.

03 · Care

Keep away from direct sunlight

Soy wax softens above 45°C. Avoid placing sachets near windows, heaters or in direct sun — heat can cause the wax to melt and potentially stain surfaces.

04 · Refresh

Warm gently to revive the scent

When the fragrance softens over time, simply rub the sachet between your palms for a few seconds. The gentle warmth reactivates the oils trapped within the wax.
